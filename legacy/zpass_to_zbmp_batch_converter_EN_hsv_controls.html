About 12‑bit / 16‑bit / 32‑bit source files
Many browser decoders down-convert images to 8-bit per channel when drawn to canvas. To keep your export “clean”, this tool:
  • Decodes true 16‑bit PNG internally (non-interlaced PNG, color types 0/2/6).
  • For other formats (including 32‑bit float EXR/TIFF), it uses the browser decoder (may be 8-bit).
